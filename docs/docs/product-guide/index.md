# LiquorPro Product Guide

Welcome to the LiquorPro Product Guide - your complete resource for understanding our liquor shop management platform.

---

## What is LiquorPro?

LiquorPro is a **complete business management solution** designed specifically for liquor retail businesses. It helps shop owners, managers, and staff run their operations smoothly - from tracking sales and inventory to managing finances and compliance.

Think of it as your **digital assistant** that handles all the complex paperwork, calculations, and record-keeping so you can focus on serving customers and growing your business.

---

## Who is LiquorPro For?

| Role | How LiquorPro Helps |
|------|---------------------|
| **Shop Owners** | Monitor all your shops from one dashboard, track profits, and make data-driven decisions |
| **Store Managers** | Manage daily operations, staff, inventory, and sales targets efficiently |
| **Cashiers/Salesmen** | Quick billing, easy returns, and customer management at the counter |
| **Accountants** | Automated financial reports, GST compliance, and bank reconciliation |
| **Delivery Staff** | Route optimization, delivery tracking, and payment collection |

## Core Features at a Glance

### 1. Point of Sale (POS)
The heart of daily operations - fast, reliable billing.

- Scan barcodes or search products
- Apply discounts and schemes
- Accept cash, card, UPI, or credit
- Print or share digital receipts
- Process returns easily

### 2. Inventory Management
Know exactly what's in stock, always.

- Real-time stock levels across all locations
- Low stock alerts before you run out
- Track expiry dates for compliance
- Transfer stock between shops
- Supplier management

### 3. Customer Management
Build relationships that drive repeat business.

- Customer profiles with purchase history
- Credit account management
- Loyalty programs and rewards
- Birthday/anniversary reminders
- Bulk SMS/WhatsApp updates

### 4. Financial Management
Keep your finances organized and compliant.

- Daily/weekly/monthly reports
- GST calculations and returns
- Bank reconciliation
- Expense tracking
- Profit & loss statements

### 5. Multi-Store Management
Manage one shop or a hundred from anywhere.

- Centralized dashboard for all locations
- Compare performance across stores
- Unified inventory view
- Role-based access control
- Franchise management

### 6. Reports & Analytics
Turn data into actionable insights.

- Sales trends and patterns
- Staff performance metrics
- Inventory turnover analysis
- Customer buying behavior
- Custom report builder
