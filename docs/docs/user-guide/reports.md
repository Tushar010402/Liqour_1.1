# Reports & Analytics Guide

## Overview

LiquorPro provides comprehensive reporting and analytics to help you understand your business performance.

---

## 1. Dashboard

### 1.1 Key Metrics

The dashboard shows:
- Today's sales total
- Items sold
- Pending approvals
- Low stock alerts
- Collection status

### 1.2 Quick Actions

Access common tasks directly from dashboard.

---

## 2. Sales Reports

### 2.1 Daily Sales Register

Detailed daily breakdown:
- Product-wise sales
- Salesman performance
- Discounts applied
- Expense summary

**Export**: PDF, Excel

### 2.2 Period Comparison

Compare sales across:
- Day over day
- Week over week
- Month over month
- Year over year

### 2.3 Top Products

View best-selling products by:
- Quantity sold
- Revenue generated
- Growth rate

---

## 3. Inventory Reports

### 3.1 Stock Status

Current stock levels across all shops:
- Available quantity
- Reserved quantity
- Reorder status

### 3.2 Stock Movement

Track all inventory changes:
- Purchases received
- Sales made
- Transfers
- Adjustments

### 3.3 Expiry Report

Monitor products approaching expiry:
- Days until expiry
- Quantity at risk
- Suggested actions

---

## 4. Finance Reports

### 4.1 Cash Flow

Daily cash movement:
- Opening balance
- Sales collections
- Expenses paid
- Closing balance

### 4.2 Collection Status

Track money collection:
- On-time collections
- Delayed collections
- Deadline breaches

### 4.3 Vendor Ledger

Vendor-wise statements:
- Outstanding balance
- Payment history
- Credit utilization

---

## 5. Export Options

### 5.1 Formats

- **PDF**: Print-ready reports
- **Excel**: For further analysis
- **CSV**: Data import/export

### 5.2 Scheduled Reports

Configure automatic report generation:
1. Go to **Reports** > **Scheduled**
2. Select report type
3. Set frequency (daily/weekly/monthly)
4. Add recipients
5. Save

---

## 6. Custom Reports

Enterprise plans can create custom reports:
- Custom date ranges
- Specific shop selection
- Product category filters
- Custom metrics

Contact support for custom report setup.
