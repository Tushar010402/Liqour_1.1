# Quick Start Guide

Get your LiquorPro system up and running in just 15 minutes. This guide covers the essential first steps.

---

## Before You Begin

### What You'll Need
- A smartphone or computer with internet
- Your shop details (name, address, GST number)
- A list of products you sell (or we'll help import)

### Estimated Time: 15 minutes

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account (2 minutes)

### For New Users

1. **Visit** the registration page or open the app
2. **Enter** your mobile number
3. **Verify** with the OTP sent to your phone
4. **Fill in** your basic details:
   - Your name
   - Email address
   - Shop name

That's it! Your account is created.

### Already Have an Account?

Simply log in with your registered mobile number and OTP.

---

## Step 2: Set Up Your Shop (5 minutes)

### Shop Profile

Fill in your shop details:

| Field | Example | Why It's Needed |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Shop Name | "Sharma Wine Shop" | Appears on receipts |
| Address | "123, MG Road, Delhi" | For delivery & compliance |
| Phone | "9876543210" | Customer contact |
| GST Number | "07AXXXX1234X1Z5" | For tax invoices |
| License Number | "L-2/2024/12345" | Excise compliance |

### Business Hours

Set your opening and closing times:
- Monday to Saturday: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed (or your hours)

### Receipt Settings

Customize what appears on receipts:
- Shop logo (optional)
- Header message ("Thank you for visiting!")
- Footer message ("Returns within 24 hours")
- Terms and conditions

---

## Step 3: Add Your Products (5 minutes)

### Option A: Quick Import (Recommended)

We have a database of 5,000+ liquor products. Simply:

1. Go to **Products** → **Import**
2. Select **categories** you sell (Whiskey, Vodka, Beer, etc.)
3. Choose **brands** you stock
4. Set your **selling prices**
5. Click **Import**

Products added with images, descriptions, and tax rates!

### Option B: Manual Entry

Add products one by one:

1. Go to **Products** → **Add New**
2. Fill in details:
   - Product name
   - Category
   - Brand
   - Size (750ml, 1L, etc.)
   - Purchase price
   - Selling price
   - Current stock
3. Click **Save**

### Option C: Bulk Upload

Have a spreadsheet? Upload it:

1. Download our template
2. Fill in your products
3. Upload the file
4. Review and confirm

---

## Step 4: Your First Sale (3 minutes)

Let's make your first sale!

### At the POS Screen

1. **Click** "New Sale" or press F1
2. **Add products** by:
   - Scanning barcode, OR
   - Typing product name, OR
   - Clicking from favorites
3. **Enter quantity** (default is 1)
4. **Select payment** method:
   - Cash
   - Card
   - UPI
5. **Complete sale** - Click "Pay" or press Enter
6. **Print/share** receipt

### Congratulations!
You've completed your first sale with LiquorPro.

## Quick Tips for Success

### Do's ✓

| Do This | Because |
|---------|---------|
| Complete every sale in the system | Accurate records |
| Update stock when receiving goods | Prevent stockouts |
| Add customer phone numbers | Build relationships |
| Check reports daily | Spot issues early |
| Back up regularly | Protect your data |

### Don'ts ✗

| Avoid This | Consequences |
|------------|--------------|
| Skipping sales in system | Wrong stock counts |
| Sharing login credentials | Security risks |
| Ignoring low stock alerts | Lost sales |
| Delaying day-end closing | Reconciliation issues |

## Common First-Day Questions

### "How do I handle a return?"
Go to **Sales** → **Returns** → Select original bill → Choose items to return → Process refund

### "What if internet goes down?"
LiquorPro works offline! Sales continue normally and sync when connection returns.

### "How do I change a price?"
Go to **Products** → Find product → **Edit** → Update price → **Save**

### "Can I cancel a sale?"
Yes, go to **Sales** → **Today's Sales** → Find the bill → **Void** (requires manager approval)

### "How do I add a discount?"
During sale, click **Discount** → Enter percentage or amount → Apply
