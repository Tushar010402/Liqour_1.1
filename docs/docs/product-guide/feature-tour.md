# Feature Tour

Explore everything LiquorPro can do for your business. This guide walks you through each feature with simple explanations and real-world examples.

---

## Point of Sale (POS)

### What It Does
Your digital cash register - but smarter. Handle all sales transactions quickly and accurately.

### Key Capabilities

#### Quick Billing
| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Barcode scanning | Bill products in seconds |
| Search by name | Find any product instantly |
| Quick-add buttons | One-tap for popular items |
| Automatic totals | No manual calculations |

#### Flexible Payments
Accept any payment method your customers prefer:

- **Cash** - With automatic change calculation
- **Cards** - Debit and credit card support
- **UPI** - Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.
- **Credit** - For trusted customers with limits
- **Split payments** - Part cash, part UPI, etc.

#### Smart Discounts
- Percentage or fixed amount discounts
- Bulk purchase discounts (buy 2 get 10% off)
- Customer-specific pricing
- Promotional schemes
- Automatic happy hour pricing

#### Returns & Exchanges
- Easy return process
- Automatic stock adjustment
- Refund to original payment method
- Exchange for different products
- Complete audit trail

### Real-World Example
> **Scenario**: A customer wants to buy 2 bottles of whiskey, pay half in cash and half in UPI, and use a 10% discount coupon.
>
> **With LiquorPro**: Scan bottles → Apply discount code → Split payment → Done in 30 seconds. Receipt sent to WhatsApp automatically.

## Inventory Management

### What It Does
Track every bottle from the moment it enters your shop until it's sold.

### Key Capabilities

#### Stock Tracking
- **Real-time levels** - Know exactly what's in stock
- **Multi-location** - Track across warehouses and shops
- **Batch tracking** - Know which batch each bottle is from
- **Expiry tracking** - Never sell expired products

#### Automatic Alerts
| Alert Type | When It Triggers |
|------------|------------------|
| Low Stock | When items fall below your set threshold |
| Out of Stock | Immediately when last item sells |
| Expiring Soon | 30/15/7 days before expiry |
| Reorder Reminder | Based on your sales patterns |

#### Stock Operations
- **Receive Stock** - Record new deliveries from suppliers
- **Transfer Stock** - Move items between locations
- **Adjust Stock** - Handle breakage, theft, or count differences
- **Stock Take** - Full inventory verification

#### Supplier Management
- Maintain supplier directory
- Track purchase history
- Compare prices across suppliers
- Set preferred suppliers per product

### Real-World Example
> **Scenario**: You have 3 shops and need to know if you have enough Johnnie Walker Black Label for a party order.
>
> **With LiquorPro**: Search "Johnnie Walker Black" → See stock at all 3 locations instantly → Transfer from high-stock location if needed.

## Smart Features

### OCR Billing (Scan & Bill)
Photograph a supplier invoice - LiquorPro automatically reads and enters all the data.

**How it works:**
1. Take a photo of the invoice
2. AI extracts product names, quantities, prices
3. Review and confirm
4. Stock updated automatically

**Saves:** 15-20 minutes per invoice

### WhatsApp Integration
- Send receipts directly to customers
- Payment reminders via WhatsApp
- Promotional messages
- Order confirmations

### Voice Commands
- "Add 2 bottles of Absolut"
- "Show today's sales"
- "Print last bill"

### Offline Mode
Works even without internet:
- Continue billing normally
- Data syncs when connection returns
- No lost transactions
