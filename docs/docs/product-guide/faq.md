## General Questions

### What is LiquorPro?
LiquorPro is a complete business management software designed specifically for liquor retail shops. It handles billing, inventory, customer management, accounting, and compliance - all from one easy-to-use platform.

### Who is LiquorPro for?
- Single liquor shop owners
- Multi-store chains
- Wine shops and beer parlors
- Liquor distributors
- Franchise operations

### Do I need technical knowledge to use LiquorPro?
No! LiquorPro is designed for everyday business users. If you can use a smartphone, you can use LiquorPro. We provide training and support to get you started.

### Is my data safe?
Absolutely. We use bank-level security:
- 256-bit encryption for all data
- Daily automatic backups
- Secure Indian data centers
- No access to your data by third parties

### Can I try before buying?
Yes! We offer a **14-day free trial** with full features. No credit card required.

Yes! LiquorPro works without internet:

Yes, we have apps for:

Absolutely. LiquorPro supports:

## Industry-Specific Questions

### Does it handle excise compliance?
Yes:
- Excise register maintenance
- Stock reconciliation reports
- License details on bills
- Inspection-ready reports

### Can I track product expiry?
Yes:
- Set expiry dates during stock entry
- Automatic alerts before expiry
- Reports of near-expiry items
- Block sale of expired items

### How do I handle breakage/theft?
Use **Stock Adjustment**:
1. Go to Inventory → Adjustments
2. Select reason (breakage, theft, damage)
3. Enter quantities
4. Add notes
5. System updates stock and logs the adjustment

### Can I set different prices for different customer types?
Yes:
- Retail price (default)
- Wholesale price
- Customer-specific pricing
- Membership tiers
